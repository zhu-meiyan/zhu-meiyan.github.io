PROJECT 01 ↗

Robotic vial
transporter.

Developed as part of undergraduate research in UIUC's Autonomous Materials Systems Group. My work included wiring and integrating the electronics, writing Arduino control code, and implementing manual controls and a basic preset vial-transfer sequence.

Arduino MegaC++Stepper MotorsSystem Integration
FIELD NOTE · A CHALLENGE I RAN INTO

Calibration took more iteration than I expected. The steps I commanded didn't always match the distance the arm actually traveled, so I tested the movement and adjusted the conversion values.

A SMALL WIN

Getting the preset sequence to move through pickup, drop-off, and home positions was one of the parts I was happiest to see working.
